# Section leads

For jobs of 6+ parts that group by ownership.

Below that, the extra layer costs more than it saves. Give each `orkestra:section-lead` one worktree, its parts' tasks, acceptance and FORBIDDEN lists, the CONSTRAINTS CHECKLIST and RUN ENVIRONMENT for every Codex brief, the two `guarded.js` commands, a `.txt` report path and a 60-minute limit. Start at most 2 sections at once. Parts that Routing gives to Claude stay with the conductor. A section lead's APPROVE never substitutes for review.

Review and close a section's parts one at a time, as for any part: `review_package.js` per part (its own BASE..HEAD), one `review-<part>.json` each, then `close_part.js`. `close_part.js` merges exactly the commits a review saw, so one review over the whole section can't close its parts separately.
