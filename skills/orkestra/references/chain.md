# Chain of command (table)

Read when a part fails, a writer or lead hits a stop condition, or it is unclear who decides.

| Level | Decides | Stops and escalates when |
|---|---|---|
| Writer (Codex, worker) | how to implement inside its brief | the same error fails twice, or the time limit |
| Section lead | order and fix rounds inside its section (≤2 per part) | a part is still failing, a file outside the section, the plan looks wrong |
| Conductor | split, routing, advisors' points, merges | design direction, scope, the user's uncommitted files, a rejected `must` |
| User | direction, scope, approvals | — |
